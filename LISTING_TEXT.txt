Long description:
Use Sliding Threshold Correlation Analysis (STCA) for polymer repeat units. Apply frozen structural rules to candidate libraries; extract readable rule families from measured data; connect independently scaled Tg, Tm, Td, TC, EC and ε-AC objectives; and export executable constraints for agent-assisted candidate design followed by explicit structural verification. The skill preserves the H-cap representation used by STCA and calls the pinned polymer-stca implementation. It requires a Python/RDKit execution environment.

Starter prompts:
1. Screen this polymer candidate table for high Tg, low EC and high TC. Choose the tier for each objective independently, then report the same-candidate intersection and failed rule conditions.
2. Extract and freeze STCA rules from this measured Td dataset, then evaluate the independent holdout and export the readable rule family.
3. Export structural constraints for low ε-AC and high thermal stability, propose candidates, and verify every proposal against the frozen rules.
